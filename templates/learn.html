Dashboard

How to Read the Engine Room
Dashboard
Dashboard
What is it, exactly?
In one sentence
The Dashboard is a real-time control room for global financial markets, updated automatically every day. Instead of checking ten different websites, everything you need to understand what's happening in the world economy is here, in one single screen.
The core idea
Financial markets are like the weather — you can't predict exactly what will happen, but you can read the signals to know whether it's sunny, about to rain, or a storm is building. The Dashboard gives you those signals, organized and calculated automatically from real market data.
What you see
Six sections, each with a specific job:

Indices — the big thermometers of global markets (S&P 500, FTSE 100, Nikkei…). When they go up, investors are confident. When they drop, they're scared or selling.

News — market-moving headlines filtered by region. A war, a central bank decision, a bankruptcy — anything that can shift prices.

Sectors — markets are split into industries (Tech, Energy, Healthcare…). Some sectors rise while others fall. Knowing which one is rising right now tells you where money is moving.

Sentiment — are large investors (funds, institutions) quietly buying or quietly selling? This signal tries to detect their moves before they show up in prices.

Macro — the economic backdrop: interest rates, dollar strength, overall market fear. These conditions shape everything else.

Portfolio — a watchlist with a clear signal for each position: Buy / Watch / Avoid, calculated from all the indicators above.
How to read it
Read top to bottom, like a funnel. Start with Macro to understand the overall mood. Check Indices to see if markets confirm that mood. Move to Sectors to find where money is flowing. Finally, Sentiment and Portfolio give you specific, actionable leads. Think of it as zooming in: global → regional → sector → individual position.
What it doesn't do
The Dashboard does not tell you what to do. It gives you organized information and calculated signals, but no financial signal is foolproof. Markets can stay irrational for a long time. This data is a reading tool, not an oracle — use it to understand better, not to bet blindly.
Updates
Data is fetched and recalculated automatically every night via a pipeline (Fetch → Compute → Render). News refreshes every hour. The timestamp shown under each section tells you how fresh the data is — anything older than 30h is worth cross-checking at the source.

Market Indices
What
12+ global benchmarks — S&P 500, Nasdaq, FTSE 100, DAX, Nikkei, Hang Seng and more. Prices are adjusted close (dividends + splits factored in) from the last completed trading day.
Columns
7-day sparkline — recent price trajectory · % Day — change vs previous close · RVOL — today's volume vs 20-day average, signed by direction · Smart Money — composite institutional signal (Accumulation / Neutral / Distribution)
Look for
Rows with a gold left border are above their 200-day moving average — structurally bullish. A green row with RVOL > 1.5 and an Accumulation signal is the strongest conviction setup. Regional divergences (USA up, EU down) often precede broader rotations.
01
Live News Feed
What
Curated macro and market headlines updated every hour, filtered into four zones: Global, USA, EU, Asia. Each article shows source, timestamp, and a two-line summary.
How to use
Hover any item to expand the full summary. Click the headline or Read full article ↗ to open the source. Articles scroll newest-first within each region.
Look for
A cluster of negative headlines in one region without a corresponding price drop in the Indices box can signal a lagged market reaction — the move may still be coming. Conversely, bad news already priced in = less downside risk.
03
Sector Rotation
What
16 sector ETFs across 4 regions (USA / EU / Asia / World). Each row shows a sparkline, a Score (momentum composite), and performance for the selected period. A — means no ETF is tracked for that sector in that region.
Tabs
Zone tabs (top) switch the region. Period tabs (bottom) switch both the performance column and the sparkline: Today · 1M · 3M · 6M · 1Y. The chart always matches the selected period.
Look for
Rows with a gold left border are above their 200-day MA — the sector has structural momentum. Rotation strategy: favour sectors entering gold-border status, reduce exposure when they lose it. Compare the same sector across zones to spot regional leadership.
04
Smart Money Sentiment
What
A composite institutional sentiment score per sector, ranging from −1.0 (Extreme Fear) to +1.0 (Euphoric). Built from three signals that institutional traders watch most: money flow, volume trend, and price structure.
Formula
MFI×40% + OBV direction×30% + Above DMA-200×30%

MFI (14d) — are buyers or sellers moving more money? · OBV direction (20d) — is cumulative volume trending up or down? · DMA-200 — is price above long-term average? Hover the ⓘ icon on any card for a breakdown.
Look for
Cross-reference with Sector Rotation. High score + positive momentum = strong conviction. High score + still-negative price = potential early accumulation before the move. Euphoric score + extended price = caution, mean reversion risk.
05
Macro Signals
What
Four macro environment indicators: Yield Curve (10Y−2Y spread), VIX (market fear), DXY (US dollar strength), and key rate spreads. Each has a signal label and a contextual note.
Yield curve
Four regimes — Steep (≥+1%) expansionary · Flat (0–1%) ambiguous · Partial Inversion (−0.5 to 0) caution, with historical false positives in 1998 & 2019 · Full Inversion (<−0.5%) recession probable, lead time 6–24 months. The signal deliberately avoids a binary alarm.
Look for
Full Inversion + VIX > 25 + widening spreads = defensive posture warranted. Steep + VIX < 15 = risk-on environment. A rising DXY tends to pressure EM equities and commodities inversely — watch Sectors / Indices for the echo.
06
Portfolio Signals
What
A custom watchlist with automated momentum signals. Each position shows a 7-day sparkline, a composite Score, and a signal: BUY · WATCH · AVOID.
Signal logic
BUY — strong positive Score + above DMA-200 · AVOID — negative Score + below DMA-200 · WATCH — mixed or borderline signals. The evidence string on each row shows exactly which factors fired (e.g. ↑DMA200 ↑Score ↑RVOL).
Look for
BUY + high RVOL + Accumulation sentiment = highest conviction setup across all three layers. AVOID + RVOL spike on a down day = potential exit or short opportunity. Signals update at each pipeline run — check the timestamp.
